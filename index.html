The artist

Featured work — The Coming Storm

Javier S.

📍 Ecuador — Available worldwide

Hi, I'm JSA — a freelance illustrator specializing in fantasy character art, with a particular love for the detailed, painterly style found in anime and video games.

I've been drawing my whole life, but took it seriously as a career in 2016 — earning a degree in Multimedia and 3D Modeling along the way. My work spans original characters, fan art, D&D commissions, and everything in between.

Whether you have a fully-fledged character concept or just a vibe, I'd love to bring them to the page. Thank you to everyone who supports my work in any way — a follow, a share, a commission. It means everything.
